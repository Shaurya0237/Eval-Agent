# IPC → BNS mapping

## Which code applies
- The **BNS replaced the IPC on 1 July 2024** (BNS section 358 repeals the IPC).
- Offence committed **on or after 1 July 2024** → charge under the **BNS**.
- Offence committed **before** → the IPC still governs it (savings clause, BNS 358(2)).
- When asked for "the section" today, answer with the **BNS** number, not the old IPC number.
